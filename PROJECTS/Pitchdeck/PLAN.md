## Draft 2-Minute Narration

**Slide 1 – Urgency & Promise (~20 s)**
“Right now frontline managers tell us their teams are tired, distracted, and rotating on and off sick leave. Gallup reports 44% of employees feel daily stress, and our product exists to flip that narrative by giving companies a proactive wellbeing operating system. Over the next two minutes I’ll show how we solve the exact talent drain you’re seeing.”

**Slide 2 – Cost of Inaction (~25 s)**
“Every year the WHO tracks 12 billion working days written off because depression and anxiety keep people home or in presenteeism mode [REF: PLAN.md#slide-outline-working]. Translate that to a 100-person company: even eight employees burning out equates to 200 lost hours a week, delayed launches, and morale spirals—before we even price the trillion-dollar global productivity leak.”

**Slide 3 – Program Pillars (~25 s)**
“We solve that gap in three integrated phases. Detect: always-on listening across workloads, pulse check-ins, and burnout triage so red flags surface early. Support: employees tap curated challenges, mindfulness tools, and same-day one-on-one consultations with licensed specialists if needed. Sustain: managers receive coaching playlists and accountability nudges so healthy habits stick and teams stay energized [REF: PLAN.md#slide-outline-working].”

**Slide 4 – Business Impact (~25 s)**
“In mid-size teams the math is clear. If 10 out of 100 employees are underperforming from burnout, that’s roughly half a million dollars in missed output at typical revenue-per-employee levels. Our pilots recover 6 of those 10 within eight weeks, reclaiming $300K and reducing turnover risk. Deloitte’s benchmark backs this with a 5-to-1 return on mental-health investment, and we feed those gains directly into your revenue and retention dashboards.”

**Slide 5 – Call to Action (~20 s)**
“Let’s launch the 12-week pilot, finalize the starter budget, and lock a Q2 review. Visit [PLACEHOLDER: website] or email jaksibek@gmail.com to align stakeholders this week. Together we’ll turn wellbeing from a reactive benefit into your competitive edge [REF: PLAN.md#slide-outline-working].”
